Core Service

The Outreach Engine Behind 6,000+ B2B Leads

Most outreach fails at the foundation — wrong data, generic copy, zero follow-through. We fix all three. Our campaigns run LinkedIn and Email simultaneously, converging on one outcome: a confirmed meeting with the right decision-maker.

LinkedIn Outreach — 200 Connection Slots a Week. None Wasted.

LinkedIn enforces a hard limit of 200 connection requests per week. That makes targeting — not volume — the entire game. Our team manually audits every prospect profile before launch.

  • 1,600+ target companies, 4,800+ manually verified prospects
  • 4-point manual profile audit — every prospect, no exceptions
  • Up to 20,000+ touchpoints across a 6-month campaign
  • Profile optimisation: headline, About section, cover photo
  • 2 LinkedIn posts per week — authority building alongside outreach
  • Full inbox management and appointment setting included
Start LinkedIn Campaign →

Email Outreach — Built to Land in the Primary Inbox.

Cold email is infrastructure before it's copywriting. We build look-alike domains with full authentication and a 14-day AI-powered warmup that builds ISP trust before a single cold message goes out.

  • Look-alike domain infrastructure — zero risk to your main domain
  • 14-day AI inbox warmup before first send
  • Triple-source data + zero-bounce email validation
  • Custom copywriting built around your ICP and value proposition
  • Parallel A/B testing: subject lines, format, and CTA variations
  • Ongoing inbox management throughout the campaign

Why Running Both Channels Together Compounds Results

LinkedIn warms the relationship. Email scales the reach. When both channels are live simultaneously, a prospect encounters your brand across multiple touchpoints — building recognition, improving reply rates, and shortening time to booked meetings.
