The Process

How It Works

A structured, 5-stage process that treats every prospect as an individual — not a number in a CRM.

1

Business Analysis

Before we write a single message, we go deep on your business. We map your ICP, identify your strongest value proposition, and understand your sales cycle — so the outreach we build is tailored, not templated.

2

Profile Optimisation

Your LinkedIn profile is your first impression. We rewrite your headline, About section, and cover photo to position you as an authority in your space — not just another vendor. Prospects should feel they want to connect before they even read your message.

3

Manual Data Sorting

We don't run your database through an automation tool and call it a day. Our team manually visits and audits every profile against a 4-point checklist: active in the last 60 days, confirmed decision-maker, company is operational, no false positives. Only then does a prospect make the list.

4

Outreach Sequencing & A/B Testing

We launch every campaign with split-tested message sequences — varying the hook, value proposition, and call to action across segments. We track acceptance and reply rates, identify what's converting, and optimise the rest of the campaign around the winning angle.

  • Invitation message — the right hook for cold connection
  • Soft sales message — value-first, not pitch-first
  • Follow-up sequence — up to 9 touchpoints per prospect
  • Rapport building — turning warm leads into booked meetings
  • InMail campaigns — reaching beyond your immediate network
5

Inbox Management & Appointment Setting

We monitor your inbox daily. Every reply is handled, every objection is addressed, and every interested prospect is moved toward a confirmed calendar slot. You only get involved when it's time to close.
