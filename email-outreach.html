The Problem

Why Most Cold Email Fails

Generic templates, shared domains with burnt reputations, unverified data, and zero follow-through. The result: spam filters, ignored messages, and wasted budget. We fix the foundation before we write a single word.

How We Fix It

Our Email Infrastructure Process

1

Domain Infrastructure

We build look-alike domains (e.g. yourbrand-growth.com) with Google Workspace inboxes, full SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication. Your main domain stays completely clean — zero deliverability risk to your primary brand.

2

14-Day AI Inbox Warmup

Before a single cold message goes out, we run a 14-day AI-powered warmup that gradually builds ISP trust. Your new domain develops a positive sending reputation — so when outreach begins, emails land in primary inboxes, not spam folders.

3

Triple-Source Data & Validation

Prospect data comes from three independent sourcing channels and is run through zero-bounce validation before the campaign launches. No guesswork, no invalid emails, no wasted sends.

4

Custom Copywriting & A/B Testing

Copy is written around your actual ICP — not pulled from a template. We run simultaneous A/B tests on subject lines, body formats (plain text, text-plus-image, HTML), and CTAs. The version that earns replies gets scaled. The rest gets cut.

5

Ongoing Inbox Management

We monitor and manage your inbox throughout the campaign. Every reply is handled, every objection addressed, every interested lead moved toward a calendar booking. You focus on closing — we handle everything before that.
