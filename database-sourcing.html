The Problem

What Bad Data Costs You

Unverified lists mean bounced emails that damage your domain reputation, LinkedIn connection requests to inactive or irrelevant profiles, wasted outreach budget, and — most importantly — missed conversations with the right buyers. The data layer is where most outreach agencies cut corners. We don't.

Our Sources

Where We Source Your Data

We don't rely on a single database. Every prospect list is built by cross-referencing multiple independent data sources and then manually verified before delivery.

Professional Platforms

  • LinkedIn Sales Navigator — live professional data
  • Apollo.io — email + phone data at scale
  • Lusha — direct contact data for decision-makers
  • Clearbit — firmographic enrichment and intent data

Manual Research

  • Company websites and leadership pages
  • Industry directories and association lists
  • Event attendee lists and conference speakers
  • Public regulatory and company registries

Validation Process

  • Zero-bounce email validation on every contact
  • 4-point LinkedIn profile manual audit
  • Active account verification (last 60 days)
  • Decision-maker title and seniority confirmation

Quality Control

  • Duplicate removal across all sources
  • Company operational status check
  • False positive elimination before delivery
  • Structured, labelled, and campaign-ready format
Customised to Your ICP
